MailRover

An indoor parcel-delivery robot developed by a five-person team at Simon Fraser University. The project combined autonomous navigation, a web application and a PIN-controlled parcel locker.

My role Team CTO — Web Application, Electronics and Systems Integration

Delivery workflow Web → Navigation → Locker
Project Overview

From a delivery request to parcel collection.

The team developed a prototype that allowed staff to register a parcel and select a predefined destination. The robot received the delivery task, navigated indoors and kept the parcel locker closed until the recipient entered the correct PIN.

01 Load Register the parcel
02 Dispatch Send the delivery task
03 Navigate Travel to the destination
04 Unlock Check the recipient’s PIN

Shared Electrical Integration

Connecting the battery-powered subsystems.

I also contributed to low-voltage power distribution and wiring as the robot’s subsystems were brought together. This included work around the Raspberry Pi, motor controller, sensors and locker electronics, alongside other team members.

  • Helped connect battery-powered subsystems and their DC power supplies.
  • Contributed to wiring and grounding between controllers, sensors and locker electronics.
  • Helped investigate power-delivery and regulator issues affecting the Raspberry Pi.
  • Checked connections and subsystem behaviour during integration testing.
