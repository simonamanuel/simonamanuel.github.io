Project Overview

Separating picture information from timing.

A composite video signal contains both picture information and the timing needed to draw it. The aim was to separate these components and produce signals suitable for controlling an oscilloscope display.

The design brought together input conditioning, synchronization, horizontal and vertical sweep generation, and brightness control. Simulation and bench measurements were used to examine how these stages behaved.

Power Supplies and Integration

Regulated rails, grounding and decoupling.

The circuit used ±12 V analog supplies and locally regulated ±5 V rails. I worked with 7805 and 7905 regulators, common grounding and local decoupling, with attention to supply noise between the timing circuitry and analog video stages.

  • ±12 V analog supply distribution
  • 7805 and 7905 voltage regulation
  • Bulk and high-frequency decoupling
  • Ground connections between circuit stages
