STM32 Industrial Sensor, DAQ & Actuator Controller

A four-layer mixed-signal PCB designed in KiCad around the STM32F103C8T6, integrating regulated logic supplies, two analog sensor channels, RS-485 connectivity and two intended DC actuator switching outputs.

Architecture

Mixed-signal interfaces on one controller PCB.

The design combines input protection, power conversion, MCU interfaces and sensor/actuator circuitry. The figures below describe design intent, not measured hardware performance.

01 / INPUT

DC Input Protection

Fused input, transient suppression and reverse-polarity protection scheme. The logic-input design range is 9–30 V DC.

02 / POWER

5 V & 3.3 V Rails

LMR36510 buck regulator followed by TLV75533 3.3 V LDO for MCU and interface circuitry.

03 / CONTROLLER

STM32F103C8T6

3.3 V microcontroller with ADC, GPIO and routed SWD, I²C, SPI and UART access.

04 / SENSING

Analog Inputs

Two nominal 0–10 V sensor channels with voltage scaling, filtering and input protection prior to MCU ADC pins.

05 / COMMUNICATIONS

RS-485 Interface

THVD1450 half-duplex transceiver with SM712 interface protection; intended for wired industrial communication.

06 / OUTPUTS

DC Actuator Switching

Two fused low-side IRLML0060 MOSFET outputs with flyback protection. Nominal 12 V / 1.5 A channel targets remain unverified.

Open Engineering Review Items

  • MOSFET gate drive: the IRLML0060 outputs are driven from approximately 3.3 V GPIO; the intended output rating has not been proven at this gate voltage.
  • Mechanical integration: connector courtyards and assembly clearances require verification.
  • Thermal review: exposed-pad via treatment and continuous-current copper-path voltage drop/temperature rise remain open.
  • No physical validation: this revision includes no tested hardware or verified firmware build.
