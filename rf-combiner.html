Project Overview

Broadband impedance matching and output-port isolation.

Compared single- and multi-section Wilkinson topologies using analytical transmission-line synthesis and EM-aware microstrip modelling, with emphasis on return loss and output-port isolation over the design band.

Design decision

Three-section transmission-line matching network.

The single-section divider established a baseline response. Cascading three impedance-transforming sections introduced additional degrees of freedom to tune match and isolation across the frequency range.

  • Compared single- versus three-section broadband S-parameter responses
  • Optimized input return loss and isolation between output ports
  • Cross-checked microstrip characteristic impedance and width using TXLINE/AWRDE
  • Documented resistor-footprint constraints affecting PCB layout completion
My Work

Transmission-line synthesis and RF circuit optimization.

Used analytical impedance calculations, MATLAB, TXLINE, and AWR Design Environment to move from an ideal transmission-line network to a substrate-specific microstrip model.

01

Initial calculations

Calculated starting characteristic impedances and electrical lengths, then translated them into initial microstrip geometry using MATLAB and TXLINE.

02

Design comparisons

Evaluated the simulated return-loss and isolation trade-offs as the Wilkinson network increased from one matching section to three.

03

Circuit optimization

Optimized transmission-line parameters in AWRDE and inspected frequency-dependent S-parameters, including matching and output-port isolation.

04

Microstrip modelling

Mapped ideal transmission lines to Rogers RO4003 microstrip widths/lengths and re-simulated the physical transmission-line representation.
