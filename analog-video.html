Project Overview

NTSC synchronization extraction and analog deflection.

Composite video multiplexes luminance and line/frame synchronization. The circuit separates these functions to drive oscilloscope X/Y deflection and Z-axis brightness modulation.

The signal chain combines input conditioning, sync separation, horizontal and vertical sweep generators, and luminance-derived intensity control. LTspice simulations and bench oscilloscope traces were used to verify timing and signal integrity.

Power Supplies and Integration

Bipolar rails, local regulation, and analog signal integrity.

Distributed ±12 V analog rails and derived regulated ±5 V supplies using 7805/7905 regulators; addressed local decoupling, common-reference grounding, and noise coupling between timing and video-processing stages.

  • Bipolar ±12 V supply distribution and return paths
  • 7805/7905 linear-regulator stages for local ±5 V rails
  • Bulk and high-frequency bypass capacitors near active circuitry
  • Ground-reference continuity across sync, sweep, and intensity stages
